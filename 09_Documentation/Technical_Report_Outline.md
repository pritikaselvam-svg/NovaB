# NovaBuds Technical Report

## 1. Introduction

Motivation for taking a headphone from concept to engineering
implementation.

## 2. Design Objectives

Ergonomics, product identity, embedded interaction, custom electronics
and compact packaging.

## 3. Industrial Design

Sketches, CAD evolution, enclosure geometry, headband, controls and
display.

## 4. Electronics

ESP32-S3, charging, regulation, audio, display, Hall sensing and
buttons.

## 5. PCB

Schematic, component selection, footprints, placement, custom outline,
routing, copper and DRC.

## 6. Firmware

Architecture and planned peripheral interfaces.

## 7. UI/UX

Ambient display and physical control concept.

## 8. Manufacturing

PCB fabrication, enclosure manufacturing and assembly.

## 9. Validation

Separate design assumptions from measured results.

## 10. Limitations

State what has not been physically tested.

## 11. Future Work

Fabrication, bring-up, firmware, acoustic testing and ergonomic testing.

## 12. Conclusion

Position NovaBuds as an integrated product-development exercise.
